A day in FeelFed

Ordinary days are what make a pattern readable.

  1. 9:00 AM
    No appetite, water only

    Energy logged at 3. Nothing eaten yet. Recorded, not flagged.

  2. 12:30 PM
    Small meal, a few bites

    Fullness climbed from 2 to 8 within ten minutes. Bloating noted.

  3. 1:15 PM
    Follow-up check-in

    Still uncomfortable. Energy unchanged. One tap, no typing required.

  4. 3:15 PM
    Short walk, ten minutes

    Energy after: 5. Marked as completed, shortened, or skipped — all equally valid.

  5. 6:00 PM
    Smoothie, finished it

    Comfortable fullness. Logged as blended. Hydration up for the day.

FeelFed adds up what you recorded and nothing else. Five entries, three textures, two symptoms, one walk. No score. No streak. No note telling you what any of it means.

Why FeelFed exists

Built because "everything looks normal" does not always tell the whole story.

FeelFed began with a simple need: a better way to document low appetite, early fullness, energy changes, movement, hydration, and the everyday experiences that are difficult to remember or explain later.

Privacy

Your experiences belong to you.

What you record in FeelFed is personal. Privacy is being considered from the first line of code.

  • Only you see your records

    No public profile, ever, by default. Nothing is shared with a doctor unless you send it.

  • Your wellness entries are not advertising data

    FeelFed will not sell your food, symptom, weight, or activity entries for targeted advertising.

  • Export it or delete it

    Take your full history with you, correct it, or remove your account entirely.

  • Plain language, no scoring

    No grades on your meals, no shame mechanics, no conclusions drawn about your body.

FeelFed is a personal wellness journal. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition, and it does not determine why you feel the way you do. It records what you tell it.
